GIRL-CHILD EDUCATION: BENEFITS, IMPORTANCE, CHALLENGES, WHAT WE CAN DO

Children are the future. Educate a man and you educate an individual. Educate a woman and you educate a nation. Although awareness is being created to educate the girl child, we realize most of these girls lack the interest to be educated and along the way fall into motherhood or bad lifestyles in search for “greener pastures.” These females then start families and transfer the level of ignorance they walk in into educating these children. That is a misguided generation already being established. We desire to go beyond that. We strategize to psyche the girl child through little competitive yet exciting programs that promote their interests and cause them to desire for them to be educated.
