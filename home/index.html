Blessed Care International

Blessed Care International (BCI) is a non-profit, non-political, and non-governmental organization.

BCI started as a home based NGO in Ghana via a group of dynamic individuals whose personal goal is to help the less privileged. Today, the vision now serves as a perfect platform to help people across the world.

Our focus is to fight hunger and lack of basic needs. Hunger is an inevitable consequence of poverty. Hunger is the most grotesque example of inequality. It is neither exciting nor good. It is ugly and must be eliminated. READ MORE

WHAT WE DO

We Educate

Education enhances our ability to grow and develop yet not everyone has the privilege of proper education. BCI assists in providing education for our “less privileged” groups of people with such need and educational materials such as learning materials, school uniforms, and renovation of their school building, food items and all that is required to make their studies effective.

WE PROVIDE FOR THE LESS PRIVILEGED

Having no one to help you in challenging times is a dire need we desire to rid people off. All fingers are not equal and whatever it takes to see a smile on the faces of the underprivileged is our joy and focus.

WE ERADICATE HUNGER

Hunger is unhealthy and an inevitable consequence of poverty. We grow food crops from our partnered farmlands which we make available for the needy within our capacity. We desire to see everyone rise above hunger. We are welcome to any support from donor agencies with like-minded visions

DISASTER MANAGEMENT

In times of natural disasters such as fire outbreaks, floods, etc, we support the victims depending on the situation. There is always a package to meet the challenge no matter its intensity.

BASIC NEEDS AND SUPPORT

According to Abraham Maslow’s hierarchy of needs, the basic needs are the first and most essential needs of man yet people are deprived of them in some parts of Africa. At BCI our passion is to see everyone we come across fulfilled and content for as long it can last them through our quota of support. We provide for the homeless; shelter, food, water, sanitation, electricity and good health.

WE EMPOWER, WE SUSTAIN PROJECTS

We empower community based organizations and the poor in rural areas to help sustain their projects. Projects are created daily for the benefit of others yet they never survive time. BCI provides information and training that equips and toughens to execute projects smoothly. Our empowerment covers project materials, financial aids and facilitators to give them good grounding.

EXCHANGE PROGRAMS

We facilitate exchange programs that bring down seasoned doctors, students, fireman, and many others from across the world to educate, support and help the less privileged. As an exchange program, we are still working on the part where we send “smart but poor children” outside the country to acquire knowledge, skills and experience different cultures.
